# Dead Space 2: VR Engine Research

Engine research toward a VR conversion of **Dead Space 2** (2011, Visceral Games):
stereo rendering and head tracking are the goal.

- **[`ENGINE-DOSSIER.md`](ENGINE-DOSSIER.md)**: the distilled, current-truth reference for this
  game's engine. Started 2026-09-13 from a first static look.
- **[`PLAYBOOK.md`](PLAYBOOK.md)**: a pointer to the reusable, engine-agnostic method, kept in
  one place in [flat-to-vr-RE-toolkit](https://github.com/TefMeister/flat-to-vr-RE-toolkit).
- **[`inbox/`](inbox/)**: hand-offs from research sessions, folded into the dossier by the
  modding session.

The blow-by-blow development history lives in [`dev-archive/`](../dev-archive/).
