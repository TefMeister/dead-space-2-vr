# dead-space-2-vr — `external-research/`

Ongoing **public research** for the Dead Space 2 VR project: leads, prior art and technique write-ups
gathered from publicly available sources, kept separate from hands-on modding work. The front door
is [INDEX.md](INDEX.md).

## Lane rules

- This folder is curated **only** by the `/gr` research session. Everyone else reads it.
- The modding session hands verdicts and new leads over as new files in [`inbox/`](inbox/), never
  by editing the index or topics here.
- Research is online only: we study what is publicly explained, credit it, and write our own code.
